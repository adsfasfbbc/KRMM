# KRMM v0.4 Novelty Ledger & Literature Positioning

| Work / Family | Problem Setup & Known Inputs | Core Method | Experimental Scope | Overlap with KRMM v0.4 | Required Differentiation Experiment |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **ROME / MEMIT / AlphaEdit** (Meng et al. 2022–2023; Fang et al. 2024) | **Oracle Editing:** Target entity, relation, and exact **gold replacement answer** are known inputs. | Closed-form rank-one / batched MLP key-value updates with null-space projection (AlphaEdit). | GPT-2 / GPT-J / Llama factual triples; efficacy, generalization, specificity. | Parameter writeback locality (`standard_kl` / `orthogonal_retain_proj`). | KRMM operates **blind** (unknown damage location, unknown gold answer) and explicitly treats oracle writeback as an upper bound rather than the method. |
| **SEA: Discovering Knowledge Deficiencies** | Automated probing to uncover factual errors / knowledge gaps in LLMs without modifying weights. | Structured probing / consistency checks across entity-relation queries. | Black-box/open-weight LLM factual deficiency discovery. | Overlaps with M2 damage discovery (`H1`). | KRMM separates **originally unknown** from **newly degraded** facts under equal query budgets on a mixed pool, and links discovery directly to downstream evidence-gated parametric repair (`End-to-End Yield`). |
| **Has this Fact been Edited?** (Youssef et al. / edit detection) | Forensic detection of whether a specific fact was previously edited in a model. | Logit/activation/behavioral signatures comparing edited vs unedited facts. | Post-edit detection on ROME/MEMIT edited models. | Overlaps with M2 reference-free anomaly detection (`H1`). | KRMM evaluates full-pool blind detection alongside `originally_unknown` and `collateral_damaged` units, then uses residual evidence graphs to decide whether to repair or abstain. |
| **RippleEdits** (Cohen et al. 2024) | Evaluates multi-hop logical consequences ("ripple effects") after oracle factual edits. | Structured knowledge-graph benchmark testing entailed vs invariant relations after editing. | Oracle-edited LLMs on multi-hop KG queries. | Directly informs KRMM Section 6.4 (`entailed_updates_passed` vs `same_entity_preserved` invariant audit). | KRMM uses residual multi-attribute constraints **before** editing to infer missing targets and gate unsafe writes, and audits both invariant and entailed relations post-repair. |
| **ThinkEval / Self-Consistency / Selective Answering** | Predicting answer correctness or deciding when to abstain at inference time without weight updates. | Reasoning consistency, verbalized/logit confidence, selective prediction risk-coverage curves. | QA / reasoning benchmarks; inference-time abstention only. | Overlaps with M4 candidate reliability (`H2`) and risk-coverage evaluation. | KRMM tests whether **minimal support-set structure** (distinguishing `redundant_distinct_paths` from `redundant_same_source` and `single_chain`) beats raw confidence/self-consistency specifically for **persistent parameter writeback** where wrong writes permanently harm the model. |
| **SEED / Knowledge Repair & Unlearning Recovery** | Recovering or auditing suppressed/unlearned knowledge in LLMs. | Relearning / probing / targeted finetuning to test whether forgotten knowledge remains accessible. | Unlearned LLMs (TOFU, WMDP, etc.), often with oracle relearning data. | Shares motivation of latent residual accessibility after damage. | KRMM forbids oracle target labels on the method side, separates `Internal-Only` from `Evidence-Augmented`, and enforces 5-gate strict locality + fresh-process reload persistence. |
