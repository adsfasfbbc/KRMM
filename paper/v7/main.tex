\begin{abstract}
Standard parametric knowledge editing assumes an oracle request naming both the corrupted fact and its target ground-truth answer. We study \emph{Blind Continual Knowledge Repair} under strict public/evaluator information isolation across four disjoint entity pools (v0.3: 140; v0.5: 52; v0.6: 50; v0.7-dev: 36) and two 3B architectures (\texttt{Qwen2.5-Omni-3B} and \texttt{Llama-3.2-3B-Instruct}). While structured evidence reconstruction outperforms generative baselines in single-target blind repair ($+23$--$53$~pp) and eliminates known wrong pseudo-label writes across 3B families ($0/22$ in v0.5), moving to \emph{true sequential multi-target continual repair} on a single shared damaged checkpoint exposes a sharp safety bottleneck: even under gold Oracle supervision ($8$ sequential \texttt{standard\_kl} LoRA merges, verified via isolated fresh Python subprocesses with $0.00$ numerical merge drift), per-fact immutable auditing detects $1$--$3$ same-entity regressions masked by group-mean accuracy gains ($Y_{\text{strict}} = 0.0$) while general commonsense benchmarks report $0.0\%$ degradation. Through controlled causal diagnosis ($D_0$--$D_4$), we rule out evaluator noise, \texttt{bf16}/PEFT merge drift ($0.00\times 10^0$), and catastrophic forgetting of prior repairs ($\text{Forget}_t = 0.00$ across all 8 steps), isolating immediate high-norm per-step collateral regressions. Finally, a matched writer matrix ($W_0$--$W_5$, $T_4$) reveals a cautionary out-of-sample result: although balanced-option generic KL ($W_1$: $Y_{\text{strict}}=0.889$, $H_{\text{ever}}=0$) and relation+history KL ($W_4/T_4$: $Y_{\text{strict}}=0.778$, $H_{\text{ever}}=0$) pass strict zero-regression on the opened v0.6 replay episode, \emph{none} of $W_0, W_1, W_2, W_4$ generalize to zero same-entity regressions on a brand-new unseen 36-entity episode (\texttt{DEV-V7}: $15/15$ targets restored, yet $1$--$3$ regressions, $Y_{\text{strict}}=0.0$). Our results establish rigorous audit standards and demonstrate that soft KL regularization alone cannot guarantee per-fact safe continual knowledge restoration.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
Parametric editors such as ROME~\cite{meng2022rome}, MEMIT~\cite{meng2023memit}, AlphaEdit~\cite{fang2025alphaedit}, GRACE~\cite{hartvigsen2023grace}, and WISE~\cite{wang2024wise} operate on oracle edit requests. We investigate the blind partial-observability regime: neither the corrupted targets nor their ground-truth answers are given, and repairs must be persisted continually into one shared model state.

\section{Problem Formulation and Per-Fact Transition Auditing}
We evaluate continual repair via per-fact non-netting state transitions across $M_0 \to M_1 \to \dots \to M_T$: target recovery $R_t$, active recovery $A_t$, previous-repair forgetting $\text{Forget}_t$, final and ever-occurred immutable harm ($H_{\text{new}}, H_{\text{ever}}$), non-target improvements $G$ (which are forbidden from offsetting $H_{\text{new}}$), and strict safe yield $Y_{\text{strict}} = R_{\text{final}} \cdot \mathbb{I}[\text{global\_safety} \land \text{fresh\_reload}]$.

\section{Causal Failure Diagnosis ($D_0$--$D_4$)}
On \texttt{Qwen2.5-Omni-3B} ($D=18$, $8$ Oracle targets, \texttt{OW-replay}):
\begin{itemize}
  \item $D_0$ (no-update): $H_{\text{new}}=0$, agreement $1.000$.
  \item $D_1$ (no-op PEFT merge $1\times, 2\times, 4\times, 8\times$): exact SHA256 match, $\max \|\Delta W\|_F = 0.00$, $\max |\Delta w| = 0.00$, $H_{\text{new}}=0$.
  \item $D_2$ (single-target from $M_0$): $8/8$ restored; $6/8$ incur $H_{\text{new}}=0$, whereas \texttt{cedar shoe tree} ($\|\Delta W\|_F=2.88$, $H_{\text{new}}=2$) and \texttt{slate chalkboard} ($\|\Delta W\|_F=3.25$, $H_{\text{new}}=2$) immediately induce collateral regressions.
  \item $D_3$ (sequential $M_0 \to M_8$): $\text{Forget}_t = 0.00$ at all 8 steps ($16/18$ restored); harm jumps $0 \to 1$ precisely at step 4 (\texttt{cedar shoe tree}) and $1 \to 2$ at step 7 (\texttt{slate chalkboard}).
  \item $D_4$ (matched 256-step joint edit): $14/18$ restored, $H_{\text{new}}=1$ ($Y_{\text{strict}}=0.0$).
\end{itemize}

\section{Limitations and Future Work}
Open-relation transfer (CounterFact/zsRE), mixed-fine-tuning damage ($D_3$), and hard-constrained/parameter-isolated continual writers (e.g., exact null-space projection or discrete routing) remain open directions (\texttt{NOT\_RUN} in Batch C1).
