## Positioning Boundary
KRMM v0.6 does **not** claim to be the first to observe residual associative knowledge (ThinkEval) or the first to perform budgeted deficiency discovery (SEA) or self-rewriting (arXiv:2611.21885). Its concrete, falsifiable contribution is the **Selective Knowledge Repair under Uncertainty** problem formulation, strict public/evaluator isolation protocol, fair detection trade-off characterization (reversed entropy vs constraint discrepancy on planned D1 vs collateral damage), and empirical demonstration of **sequential cumulative same-entity interference** under fresh-subprocess auditing.
